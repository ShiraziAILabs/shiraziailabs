Research / Biomedical

Cancer & biology

Computational research questions around cancer dependencies, stress response and testable biological hypotheses.

Questions I’m exploring

Which gene dependencies are shared or context-specific? What mechanisms could bypass a candidate target? How can public biological datasets help prioritize experiments?

Datasets and approach

Exploratory work with public CRISPR fitness data and single-cell datasets, emphasizing confounders, reproducibility and cautious interpretation.

Scope

This work is computational and exploratory. It does not establish a therapy, clinical efficacy or medical advice.

Biological hypotheses suggested by public data require independent experimental validation.
