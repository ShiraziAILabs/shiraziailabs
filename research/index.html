THE RESEARCH LIBRARY

questions at its center.

A cross-disciplinary collection of investigations. Public descriptions focus on research questions and scope; formal publication and validation status are distinguished from work in progress.

01 / MATHEMATICS

Structure hidden in simple questions.

Research directions include one-missing-distance turnpike reconstruction, graceful and near-graceful graph labeling, difference systems, and bounded-collision additive structures. The core interest is understanding when a collection of constraints uniquely determines an object—and when it does not.

Discrete mathematicsCombinatoricsReconstructionProof verification

Working manuscripts and research notes are not represented here as independently peer-reviewed results. Verified publication links can be added individually.

02 / BIOMEDICAL

Reading signals within biological complexity.

Computational inquiries examine pancreatic beta-cell stress responses, expression-state changes, MAP3K15/MAP3K5-associated pathways, and cancer-cell gene dependencies. The goal is to move from observations toward carefully testable hypotheses—not to claim clinical efficacy from exploratory data.

Single-cell analysisER stressFunctional genomicsHypothesis testing

Exploratory research; not medical advice, a clinical result, or a validated therapeutic claim.

03 / PHYSICS & ASTRONOMY

What can observations really tell us?

Explorations span phase retrieval, inverse reconstruction, spectroscopic and interferometric measurements, and astronomical signal analysis using public datasets. Emphasis is placed on distinguishing true structure from ambiguity, artifacts, and competing explanations.

Inverse problemsPhase retrievalTESS dataSignal validation

Research directions and analysis workflows; no claim of a confirmed celestial discovery.
