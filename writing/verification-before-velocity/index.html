AI ENGINEERING · FIELD NOTE

Verification before velocity

Fast AI-generated outputs are valuable only when the important claims can survive independent checks.

QSQasim Shirazi
Shirazi AI Labs · Field notes

The attraction of speed

Models can draft code, summarize technical literature, and propose approaches extraordinarily quickly. But an answer that arrived in seconds can still be wrong in a way that is difficult to notice.

Treat outputs as claims

A useful engineering pattern is to turn each consequential output into something testable: an assertion, a reproducible computation, a source reference, a unit test, or a documented decision. The specific form depends on the domain.

Look for failure, not just success

The best evaluation asks when a system breaks. What data was held out? Which alternative explanations remain? What happens when input is incomplete? An honest record of failure is often more valuable than a polished demo.

A practical mindset

Verification is not the opposite of creative AI work. It is how creative work becomes more dependable and useful to others.
