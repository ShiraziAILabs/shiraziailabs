AI engineering / Field note

Verification before velocity

Fast AI output is useful. Independent checks make it dependable.

Generation is only a first step

AI can draft code, suggest research hypotheses and explain difficult material at remarkable speed. But a plausible answer isn't the same thing as a tested answer.

Make checks visible

In software, run tests and compare with reliable baselines. In mathematics, replay certificates or establish proof obligations. In scientific work, distinguish exploratory patterns from established evidence.

The goal

Use automation to increase productivity while keeping validation, uncertainty and reproducibility in the workflow.
