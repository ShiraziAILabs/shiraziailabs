The art of the technical demo

The best demonstrations answer the buyer's questions, rather than showing every feature.

Start with discovery

Before opening the product, understand the customer's workflow, constraints and decision criteria.

Show a relevant journey

Organize the demo around a believable task and outcome. Use examples that resemble the buyer's real world instead of walking through every menu.

Make the decision easier

A strong demo leaves room for integrations, limitations, security and the practical reality of implementation. The aim is clarity, not spectacle.
